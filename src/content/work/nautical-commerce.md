At Nautical Commerce I wrote for two audiences at once: developers integrating with the API, and non-technical merchants running a marketplace from the dashboard.

## What I worked on

- **Feature documentation.** I documented new features across the Nautical Dashboard, API, and Storefront.
- **Release notes.** I published weekly release notes covering platform enhancements, bug fixes, and feature updates.
- **Self-service help.** I wrote FAQs and help guides in Intercom for non-technical users.
- **API work.** I tested GraphQL APIs and helped refine their request and response formats. I also documented the internal REST APIs used to test beta features, working in Postman.
- **Visual explanations.** I built wireframes and diagrams in Figma and Lucidchart that made complex workflows easier to follow and shortened onboarding.
- **Tutorial videos.** I produced tutorial videos with AI tools including Gemini, ChatGPT, Canva, and ElevenLabs.

## Choosing the platform by what it could measure

I set up Google Analytics on the Docusaurus help guides to track developer engagement. Later I migrated the dashboard and storefront documentation from Docusaurus to Intercom, because Intercom gave better analytics on how users moved through the content.

## Feedback into the product

I worked with product, engineering, and customer success on long-running, concurrent projects in Linear, and fed user feedback back into platform improvements.
