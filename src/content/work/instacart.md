I joined the Instacart Developer Platform documentation team on a six-month contract.

## What I worked on

- **Developer Platform docs.** I documented new features, and worked on product documentation, concept pages, and API endpoints for the public and private APIs.
- **Tutorials.** I wrote how-to guides and tutorials on integrating the Instacart Developer Platform into existing applications.
- **Instacart Widgets.** I improved and maintained the existing Widgets documentation, using a CI/CD workflow to review, update, and deploy content.
- **Release notes.** I worked on the format for release notes and changelogs on the platform's public site.
- **API testing.** I tested Developer Platform APIs and helped improve their request and response formats.

## Measuring and listening

I set up Microsoft Clarity on the Docusaurus-powered developer portal to track how developers engaged with the docs, and worked with product and engineering to act on user feedback about the APIs.

## Inside the team

I migrated the documentation team's internal knowledge base from Google Docs to Confluence, and designed wireframes in Figma and Lucidchart to support the developer documentation. Projects ran in Jira alongside product, engineering, and customer success.
